OUR STORY

More Than Just A Restaurant

Hotel Chandikhol is built around one simple idea: good food makes people happy.

We offer a variety of Indian dishes prepared using fresh ingredients and traditional flavours. Our goal is to provide delicious food in a comfortable and family-friendly environment.

Whether you are stopping by for a quick meal, enjoying dinner with family or meeting friends, Hotel Chandikhol welcomes you.
